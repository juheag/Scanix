Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Scanix app (the "App") for iPhone and iPad handles, where it is stored and what control you have over it. Scanix is designed so that your documents stay with you: we have no servers of our own and no user accounts, and we never see or receive your documents.

1. Controller

Julián Hernández Aguirre, independent developer, Colombia.
Contact: juheag@icloud.com

2. Information the App handles

  • Documents you scan or import (page images and PDFs), their names, folders and the text recognized in them.
  • Signatures and fingerprints you place on documents or save for reuse. Fingerprints are images of ink on paper; the App does not create or store biometric templates.
  • Edits you make on pages (text, drawings, highlights, white-out).
  • Preferences such as language, list view or sort order.

All of this is stored on your device and, if you have Scanix Premium, in your private iCloud account (see section 4).

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents and signatures or fingerprints on paper.
  • Photos: only when you choose to import an image from your library.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to protect the use of your saved signatures and fingerprints. iOS performs the check; the App only receives a "yes" or "no" and never accesses your biometric data.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings.

4. iCloud (Scanix Premium)

With Scanix Premium, your documents, signatures, fingerprints and folders are synced to the private database of your iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. That information is protected by Apple and tied to your Apple ID: we have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

You can delete that data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Scanix.

5. Text recognition

Text recognition (for search and export to Word or Excel) uses Google ML Kit and runs on your device: images of your documents are not sent to anyone. ML Kit may send Google technical information about how it works (performance and feature usage), without the content of your documents. More information: ML Kit terms.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Scanix Premium payments are processed by Apple through your Apple ID; we do not receive your payment details. To manage your subscription we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier generated by the App and your purchase information (product, date, subscription status). More information: RevenueCat privacy policy.

7. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, AdMob may collect device data such as the advertising identifier, IP address (approximate location) and how you interact with ads. iOS will ask whether you allow tracking: if you don't, ads will not be personalized. Scanix Premium shows no ads. More information: how Google uses data in advertising.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't have servers that store your documents.
  • We don't read, analyze or share the content of your documents.

9. Retention and deletion

Information stays as long as you keep it in the App. You can delete documents, signatures and fingerprints from the App at any time. Uninstalling the App deletes what is stored on the device; what is stored in iCloud is deleted as described in section 4.

10. Your rights

Under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and other applicable laws, you have the right to access, update, correct and delete your personal data, and to withdraw your consent. Because your information is on your device and in your iCloud, you can exercise most of these rights directly from the App and your iPhone Settings. For any question or complaint, write to juheag@icloud.com.

11. Children

Scanix is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date.
